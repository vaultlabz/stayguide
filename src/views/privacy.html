Privacy Policy

Effective {{effective_date}}

StayGuide is run by {{legal_name}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the StayGuide service collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: {{contact_email}}.

Who this covers

  • Customers: hosts and property management companies with a StayGuide account, and the admins they invite.
  • Crew: cleaners, maintenance staff and vendors a customer invites to the Ops app.
  • Guests: people staying at a customer's property who use the in-home tablet or the phone guide link.

For crew and guest information, the customer (the host or company) decides what is collected and why; we process it on their behalf. Ask them first about information they hold about you. We still answer requests sent to us.

What we collect

Customers and admins

  • Name, email, company name and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Property content you enter: addresses, Wi-Fi details, house rules, door and access codes (access codes are stored encrypted), photos and videos, local recommendations, booking and review links.
  • Billing: plan, invoices and payment status. Card and bank details are entered on Stripe's pages and stay with Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.
  • The date you accepted these terms and which version.

Crew (Ops app)

  • Name, email, optional phone and business details (for vendors, insurance certificates you upload).
  • Jobs, checklist results, notes, photos and time entries.
  • Location: when you check in or out of a job, your phone's location at that moment and its distance from the property are saved with the time entry. We do not track location in the background or between check-ins.
  • A push notification subscription if you turn notifications on.

Guests

  • No account and no sign-in. Using the tablet or phone guide does not require any personal details.
  • Anonymous usage counts: which guide sections were opened and booking or review codes scanned. These are not linked to a name, email or device identifier of yours.
  • If the host turned it on, an "Are you at the property?" question; we store only the yes/no answer, not your location.
  • If you report an issue: the description and any name, room or phone number you choose to add, shared with the host so they can respond.

Everyone

  • Server logs: IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. Login links and guide link codes are masked in logs. Logs are used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Your browser stores your sign-in token and display preferences (such as light or dark mode). We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the service: show guides, route guest reports and jobs, send the emails and notifications you asked for, and bill subscriptions.
  • To show customers how their guides are used and how many guests scanned their direct booking links.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To tell customers about changes to the service or their account.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The customer whose property, team or guide the information relates to.
  • Service providers that run parts of the service for us: our hosting provider; Stripe (payments); our email provider (account and alert emails); browser push services (Ops notifications); Open-Meteo (weather and place search; receives the property's address or coordinates, not personal details).
  • Content a host adds: videos from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) or Vimeo, images and map links load from those services when a guest opens them, and those services apply their own policies.
  • Direct booking and review links take you to the host's own website or a review site. Their privacy policies apply there. The link carries a random click code and campaign tags so the host can count bookings from StayGuide; it carries no personal information.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect people's safety or our rights.
  • A business transfer, such as a merger or sale, under this policy.

How long we keep it

  • Account and property information: while the account is open, and deleted or anonymized within 90 days after it is closed, except what we must keep for tax and accounting (invoices).
  • Crew time entries and job history: as long as the customer keeps the account, because they may need them for payroll and records.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.
  • Backups roll over within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Customers can edit or delete their property content at any time in the dashboard, and can ask us to close the account and delete its data.
  • Anyone can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about them, or ask how it is used, by writing to {{contact_email}}. We may need to confirm who you are and, for crew or guest information, involve the customer.
  • Crew can turn off location permission in the phone's settings; check-in still works without it, but the host sees that no location was recorded.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have further rights under local law; we honor them.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, property access codes are encrypted, and staff access to data is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

StayGuide accounts are for adults running a business. Guests of any age may view a guide, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above, and for significant changes we email customers before the change takes effect.
