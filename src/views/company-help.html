Properties

  1. On the dashboard, click Add New Property.
  2. Fill in the Basic Information, WiFi & Access Information and Guest Instructions (check-in, check-out, house rules, emergency contact).
  3. Upload a Main Property Image. It is the photo guests see first.
  4. Add Property Amenities so guests know what the home offers.

Click View Tablet on a property to see exactly what the tablet shows.

Guide content

Open a property and scroll to Guide content. Each tab is a section of the guest guide:

  • Welcome: a short greeting shown on the home screen.
  • Announcements: time-limited notices, such as pool maintenance (Pro).
  • Restaurants and Local info: your recommendations nearby.
  • How-to videos: YouTube or Vimeo links that play inside the guide.

Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to reorder items. Changes reach tablets within 15 minutes, or right away after a reload.

Tablets

Tablets are a Pro feature. Every tablet opens the same address, /tablet, and is linked to one property by pairing.

  1. Open the property, go to Tablets, optionally type a name (for example Living room) and click Pair new tablet.
  2. A 6-digit code appears. It works once and expires after 15 minutes.
  3. On the tablet's pairing screen, enter the code and tap Pair Tablet.
  4. The guide loads, and the dashboard shows the tablet as Last seen just now.

A lost or replaced tablet: click Revoke. It loses access and clears its copy of the property on its next connection. To move a tablet, revoke it at the old property and pair it at the new one.

For kiosk lock-down (Fully Kiosk Browser), mounting and the pre-departure checklist, ask support for the kiosk setup guide.

Guest link (phone guide)

Each property has a private link and QR code that opens the guide on a guest's phone, with no app to install. Find it under Guest link (phone guide) on the property.

  • Choose whether the Wi-Fi password appears on the link.
  • Rotate link makes the old link and QR code stop working, for example after a guest you no longer trust has stayed.

Guest reports

Guests can report a problem from the tablet or the guest link. Open reports show under Guest reports on the dashboard. Set each one's status as you work on it, and mark it resolved or closed once handled. Urgent reports are also emailed to your company address.

Plans & billing

  • Free: one property with the phone guide link.
  • Pro: billed per property; adds tablets, themes, announcements and analytics.
  • Portfolio: Pro for every property, billed per block of 5.

Change plan, update your card or download invoices from Billing. Removing a property stops its billing at the end of the cycle.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
"Invalid or expired pairing code"Generate a new code. After 10 wrong attempts from one network, pairing pauses for 15 minutes.
Tablet shows the pairing screen againIt was revoked or its storage was cleared. Pair it with a new code.
"You're offline" banner on the tabletThe tablet keeps showing its saved copy. Fix the Wi-Fi; it reconnects on its own.
"Last seen" is hours oldCheck the tablet's power and Wi-Fi, then reload or restart it.
Content changes are not showingWait up to 15 minutes, or reload the tablet.
